CURRENT CONFIGURATION ANALYSIS
THEME

► NIGHT swaps in a dark track with neon-lit glowing gates, closer to a real night-time FPV race — lighting is tuned to stay readable even in the dark. Trees default to off in this theme (flip "Show trees" below if you want them back), and each theme remembers that choice separately. Switches instantly.

GRAPHICS QUALITY

► Drives render resolution, antialiasing and the shadow map together. Drop to LOW on a phone or an integrated GPU if the frame rate stutters — it costs shadows but roughly halves the work per frame. Defaults to MEDIUM on touch devices, HIGH on desktop.

LAP TIMING
Best lap: —

► The timer starts when you cross the first gate and posts a lap each time you complete all gates in order. A lap in which you crash is marked in red and can't set a new best — the best time and the running +/- delta persist across sessions.

WORLD GENERATION

► When on, pressing R reshuffles gates and trees along with your position, like it always has. Turn it off to keep the same layout — pressing R will only reset your position, and you can reshuffle the layout manually with the button.

FPV HUD OVERLAY

► Draws a horizon bar across the centre of the view that tilts with roll and slides with pitch, flanked by two vertical gauges whose dots track the current pitch and roll angles. Turn it off for a clean, uncluttered FPV feed.

SCENERY

► Turns the trees dotted around the map on or off. Hidden trees never cause a collision, regardless of the "Collide with trees" setting below.

COLLISIONS

► When enabled, touching a gate's frame/pole or a tree triggers a crash. Turn either off to fly straight through that obstacle type — handy for practicing lines without being punished for clipping scenery.
