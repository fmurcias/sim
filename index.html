LENS & SHUTTER

► FPV WIDE bends straight lines toward the frame edges like the ~150° lens on a real FPV camera; the FOV setting above still controls how wide the centre of the image is. Motion blur follows a real shutter: racers fly without filters (fast shutter, crisp), freestyle pilots add ND filters to slow the shutter down for smooth, blurred cinematic footage.

IMAGE
Camera shakemotor vibration and propwash wobble — 0 turns it off 35%

► Auto exposure meters the scene like a real camera: pitch up into a bright sky and the ground darkens, dive into shade and the image opens up (with more sensor noise). ANALOG simulates a 5.8 GHz analog video link — lower resolution, colour smear, and static that gets worse with range from the launch pad and when trees block the line of sight. These camera effects need MEDIUM quality or higher.

ENVIRONMENT

► Each preset is a real photographed HDR sky (Poly Haven, CC0): it lights the whole scene, sets where the sun is (and so the shadows), and tints the distant haze. GOLDEN HOUR puts the sun low for long shadows and glare; OVERCAST gives soft, shadowless light; NIGHT is moonlit, with the gate LEDs and camera noise doing the rest. Each preset remembers its own "Show trees" choice.

GRAPHICS QUALITY

► Drives resolution, anti-aliasing, shadow cascades, ambient occlusion, the camera effects and grass density together. MEDIUM and HIGH also lower the render resolution on their own when the frame rate drops. ULTRA is for a discrete GPU; drop to LOW on a weak phone — it skips the post-processing entirely. Defaults to LOW. Press P while flying for a performance readout.

LAP TIMING
Best lap: —

► The timer starts when you cross the first gate and posts a lap each time you complete all gates in order. A lap in which you crash is marked in red and can't set a new best — the best time and the running +/- delta persist across sessions.

WORLD GENERATION

► MAP appears when captured real-world maps are installed (assets/maps/maps.json) — photographic 3D scans with their own gate positions; FIELD is the procedural flying field. When on, pressing R reshuffles gates and trees along with your position, like it always has. Turn it off to keep the same layout — pressing R will only reset your position, and you can reshuffle the layout manually with the button.

FPV HUD OVERLAY

► Draws a horizon bar across the centre of the view that tilts with roll and slides with pitch, flanked by two vertical gauges whose dots track the current pitch and roll angles. Turn it off for a clean, uncluttered FPV feed.

SCENERY

► Turns the trees dotted around the map on or off. Hidden trees never cause a collision, regardless of the "Collide with trees" setting below.

COLLISIONS

► When enabled, touching a gate's frame/pole or a tree triggers a crash. Turn either off to fly straight through that obstacle type — handy for practicing lines without being punished for clipping scenery.
